Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

CSR Desk ("we", "our", "us") provides a customer support messaging tool that lets businesses receive and respond to customer messages from channels such as Facebook Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp, and website live chat. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

  • Contact information you or a customer provides when starting a conversation, such as name, email address, and phone number.
  • Your public profile name from Facebook or Instagram when you message a connected Page, as permitted by Meta's Platform Terms, so our agents know who they are speaking with.
  • The content of messages exchanged between customers and support agents (or our AI assistant) through connected channels.
  • Basic account information for support agents who use the dashboard, such as name, email, and login credentials.

How We Use Information

  • To display and respond to customer conversations in the support dashboard.
  • To generate automated replies through a connected AI assistant, when enabled by the business.
  • To maintain a record of past conversations for support continuity and quality purposes.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Message content may be shared with a third-party AI service (such as Dify and its underlying language model provider) solely to generate an automated reply, when a business has enabled that feature. We may also share information with Meta Platforms, Inc. as required to operate Messenger, Instagram, and WhatsApp integrations, in accordance with Meta's Platform Terms and Developer Policies.

Data Retention

Conversation and contact data is retained for as long as needed to provide support services, or until the business operating this tool deletes it.

Your Choices

You may ask the business you are messaging to delete your conversation history or contact information by reaching out through the same channel.

Children's Privacy

This service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the Facebook Page or website associated with this service.
